Designing an event platform that supports both promotion and registration

Cosmeticon 2023 was a redesign proposal for the official website of a regional medical convention. The project covered both desktop and mobile experiences, with the goal of presenting the event, its speakers, schedule and registration process through a clear and modern interface.

The existing website contained a large amount of information but lacked visual hierarchy, making it difficult for visitors to quickly understand the event or locate important content. The redesign focused on improving organization while preserving all essential information.

The final interface balances promotional content with practical information, creating a smoother experience for visitors while keeping registration accessible throughout the browsing journey.

Project Goals

The website needed to communicate:

  • event information
  • speakers
  • schedule
  • sponsors
  • pricing
  • registration
  • checkout

while remaining simple to navigate.

The main objective was to improve information hierarchy so visitors could easily understand the event and reach registration without searching through long sections of content.

This shifted the focus from creating attractive layouts to designing a clear information hierarchy.

Check the full prototype

The Challenge

The convention website served several purposes at once: promoting the event, presenting speakers, providing practical information and allowing users to register online.

Organizing these different types of content into a single landing page required careful prioritization to avoid overwhelming visitors while still making every section easy to access.

The design process focused on improving clarity, navigation and readability without removing important information from the original structure.

design approach

The project began with an analysis of the existing website to identify usability issues and opportunities for improving the overall experience.

Wireframes were used to reorganize the page structure before moving into high-fidelity layouts, ensuring that content hierarchy was validated early in the process.

Visual elements, typography and spacing were then refined to create a cleaner interface that reflected the event's professional identity while remaining approachable and easy to navigate.

Structure & Layout

The landing page was reorganized into clear sections that guide visitors naturally through the event, from introduction to registration.

Information was grouped according to user priorities, allowing attendees to quickly find schedules, speakers, pricing and registration without unnecessary navigation.

The page includes:

  • Hero section introducing the convention
  • Featured speakers
  • Event schedule
  • Registration and ticket information

Spacing, typography and visual grouping were used to reduce cognitive load and make large amounts of information easier to scan.

Each section follows a consistent visual rhythm, helping users maintain orientation as they move through the page.

The responsive layout preserves the same information hierarchy across desktop and mobile devices, ensuring a consistent experience regardless of screen size.

Clear calls to action were placed throughout the page so visitors could register whenever they had enough information, without interrupting the natural reading flow.
