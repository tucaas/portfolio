Designing and scaling a premium ecommerce experience for Bravo Energy.

Bravo Energy is an energy pouch brand with a growing ecommerce presence. As part of the design team at Solo Media Group (SMG), I contributed to the redesign and expansion of the Shopify storefront, creating new interfaces, reusable components, and visual assets while ensuring consistency across a large collection of customer-facing pages.

Working alongside a Project Manager and developers, I translated project requirements into production-ready UI, helping extend the existing design system while maintaining a cohesive experience throughout the site.

Project Scope

Rather than creating a website from scratch, this project focused on expanding and refining an established e-commerce platform while maintaining a cohesive user experience and visual language.

Throughout the project, I designed and delivered interfaces across multiple areas of the platform, including:

  • Homepage and promotional landing sections
  • Bundle Builder experience
  • Product listing and collection pages
  • Product Detail pages
  • Shopping cart and checkout touchpoints
  • Supporting marketing pages and reusable content modules
  • Responsive mobile adaptations across the platform

Alongside these interfaces, I expanded the platform's component library and produced supporting visual assets, ensuring new features integrated seamlessly with the existing design system while remaining scalable for future development.

Check the website

Design System

Instead of treating each page as an isolated design, the project relied on a scalable component-based approach.

My work focused on expanding the existing design system by creating reusable interface elements, defining consistent spacing and typography patterns, producing new illustrations and graphical assets, and ensuring every new section integrated seamlessly with the established visual language.

This approach allowed the storefront to grow without sacrificing consistency or usability.

UI Design

The interface balances bold branding with ecommerce best practices, giving promotional content, product discovery, and purchasing equal attention.

Layouts were designed to improve readability, support merchandising, and guide users naturally between browsing, learning about products, and completing purchases. Particular attention was given to hierarchy, whitespace, and modular content blocks that could be reused across multiple templates.

Collaboration

This project was developed as part of a multidisciplinary team at Solo Media Group.

While the overall product direction and priorities were defined by the Project Manager, I was responsible for translating those requirements into polished production-ready interfaces, collaborating closely with developers throughout implementation to ensure design quality and consistency across the site.

Outcome

The final result is a scalable Shopify storefront that combines a consistent visual language with flexible page templates capable of supporting future product launches, marketing campaigns, and content updates.

By expanding the design system rather than designing isolated screens, the project established a foundation that allows the brand to evolve while maintaining a cohesive user experience.
