Designing a scalable e-commerce platform for vinyl enthusiasts

Vertigo Vinyl is a responsive e-commerce platform developed at Solo Media Group (SMG). As the UI Designer on the project, I was responsible for designing the user interface across the platform, transforming business requirements and creative direction into a cohesive, production-ready design system.

Working closely with the project manager and development team, I contributed to the design of the complete shopping experience, ensuring consistency across every touchpoint while balancing commercial goals with an interface that reflects the culture and collectability of vinyl records.

The project spans multiple user journeys—from discovery and exploration to purchase and customer support—and was designed for both desktop and mobile devices.

Understanding the Problem

As UI Designer, I was responsible for the visual execution of the platform throughout the project lifecycle.

My responsibilities included:

  • Translating product requirements into interface solutions
  • Designing responsive layouts for desktop and mobile
  • Developing reusable UI components
  • Maintaining visual consistency across dozens of screens
  • Creating interaction patterns for navigation and shopping flows
  • Collaborating with the project manager to iterate on features and layouts
  • Preparing designs for developer handoff

Although product direction and priorities were defined collaboratively, I owned the interface design and visual consistency across the platform.

Project Scope

Unlike a marketing website or landing page, Vertigo Vinyl required a complete e-commerce ecosystem capable of supporting different user journeys and a growing product catalog.

The project included the design of:

  • Homepage
  • Mega menu
  • Search mechanisms
  • Shop pages
  • Product Detail pages
  • Artists and Label pages
  • Cart
  • FAQ
  • About
  • Contact

Each page was designed as part of a unified experience rather than as isolated screens, allowing users to move seamlessly between browsing, discovering artists, purchasing products, and accessing support.

Design System

Because the platform contains a large number of templates and recurring interface patterns, establishing a scalable design system became a core part of the project.

Reusable components, typography rules, spacing standards, button styles, cards, navigation elements, forms, and product modules were designed to create consistency while allowing different sections of the website to evolve independently.

This modular approach streamlined design decisions, improved consistency across pages, and simplified collaboration with developers during implementation.

Information Architecture

One of the project's primary challenges was organizing a large amount of content without overwhelming users.

The platform combines editorial content, artist profiles, exclusive collections, product catalogs, labels, customer information, and purchasing flows—all of which required a clear navigation structure.

To support this, the interface emphasizes:

  • Progressive disclosure of information
  • Clear visual hierarchy
  • Consistent navigation patterns
  • Predictable page layouts
  • Fast access to key shopping actions

The result is an experience that accommodates a large volume of content while remaining approachable and easy to navigate.

Responsive Experience

Every feature was designed for both desktop and mobile from the beginning of the project.

Rather than simply adapting layouts, each breakpoint was considered independently to preserve usability, readability, and interaction quality across devices while maintaining a consistent visual language.

Outcome

Vertigo Vinyl demonstrates my ability to contribute to large-scale commercial products within a collaborative environment.

Beyond designing individual screens, the project required maintaining consistency across an extensive interface, designing reusable systems instead of isolated pages, and balancing business requirements with user-centered interface design.

Working within an established product team strengthened my ability to communicate design decisions, collaborate across disciplines, and build interfaces that are both visually cohesive and ready for development.
