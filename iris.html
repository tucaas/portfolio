Building a cohesive digital identity for an independent tattoo artist

Íris Palo is a tattoo artist and illustrator based in Florianópolis, Brazil. She approached me to create a complete visual identity and website that better represented her work while providing a professional online presence for potential clients.

The project combined branding, UX/UI design and front-end development, resulting in a consistent identity across digital touchpoints. From logo creation to the final responsive website, every element was designed to reflect her artistic style while making information easier to access.

Beyond showcasing completed tattoos, the website also helps visitors understand the artist's process, services and booking workflow, making the first contact feel more approachable.

Project Scope

This project included:

  • Brand identity
  • Logo design
  • Design system
  • Website design
  • Responsive layouts
  • Front-end development

The challenge wasn't simply creating an attractive portfolio. The goal was to build an identity that communicated professionalism without losing the handcrafted personality that defines the artist's work.

Git page

The Challenge

The artist relied primarily on social media to showcase her work, making it difficult to communicate her process, services and brand identity in a structured way.

The website needed to function as both a portfolio and a business tool, helping visitors discover the artist's work, understand how appointments worked and feel confident enough to reach out.

The challenge was balancing expressive visuals with clear navigation so the artwork remained the focus without sacrificing usability.

Design Approach

The project began by defining a visual language inspired by the artist's illustration style and tattoo work. Rather than following common minimalist tattoo studio aesthetics, the identity embraces decorative elements and organic shapes that make the brand immediately recognizable.

Once the overall direction was established, branding assets and interface components were developed together to ensure the visual language remained consistent across every touchpoint.

This approach allowed branding and UI decisions to reinforce each other instead of being treated as separate deliverables.

Brand Identity

The logo was custom designed specifically for the project, with every letter refined individually to create a distinctive visual identity.

Typography, proportions and decorative details were carefully balanced so the mark remained readable while reflecting the handcrafted nature of the brand.

Multiple logo variations were created to support different applications including social media, website headers, printed materials and promotional assets.

Different logo variations were created to ensure flexibility across multiple contexts, including social media, banners, profile pictures, and headers.

These variations allow the brand to remain consistent while adapting to different formats and sizes.

Design System

A lightweight design system was developed alongside the branding to maintain consistency throughout the website.

Color, typography, reusable interface components and decorative assets were documented so new pages and future materials could follow the same visual language.

Illustrations, icons and ornamental graphics derived from the logo help strengthen the identity without competing with the tattoo artwork itself.

Web Design

The website was designed to guide visitors naturally from discovering the artist's work to understanding her process and booking a session.

Content was organized into clear sections, introducing the artist, showcasing featured projects, answering common questions and providing multiple opportunities for users to make contact.

Large imagery, generous spacing and subtle interactions allow the artwork to remain the primary focus while reinforcing the overall brand identity.

The final project delivers a cohesive digital presence that combines branding, UX/UI design and development into a single experience. The website reflects the artist's personality while providing potential clients with a clearer understanding of her work, process and services.
